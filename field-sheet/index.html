      Limits
      
        
      
          
      
            The sheet applies Eq. 1 to Eq. 9 from Zure, Kuo and Drizo,
            Chemosphere 2024;351:141101.
            doi:10.1016/j.chemosphere.2023.141101
          
      
          
      It does not measure a sample. It does not invent a titer.
      
          
      
            Use one unit for the spike, the water and the plant totals. A PFU is not a gene copy. Eq. 9
            reports viral particles per mL. Those particles stay out of the closure unless you mark the part.
          
      
          
      Eq. 2 divides by the dilution you type. A thousand-fold dilution is 0.001.
      
          
      
            Blank decay comes off the spiked total before the closure. That is the herb-free control in the
            paper.
          
      
          
      
            A positive residual is spike that is not in the water or the parts. A negative residual means the
            water and the parts add up to more than the adjusted spike.
          
      
          
      
            The MS2 example uses illustrative part totals from the worked example in this repository. It is
            not a new measurement.
          
      
          
      Records stay in this browser. There is no account. Clearing site data deletes the journal.
