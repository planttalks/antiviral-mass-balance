Pre-harvest mass balance

Initial spike

Natural decay, Equation 1

Bulk water at harvest, Equation 6

Phytoremediation efficiency is shown after the initial and final water concentrations are entered.

Genome constants for Equation 9

MS2 uses 3569 base pairs and a mass term of 330. T4 uses 169000 base pairs and a mass term of 660. Change these values only for a different virus.

Harvested plant parts

Equations 2 and 3 convert plaque counts to an infective total. If that total is already known, leave the plaque fields empty. Equation 9 is excluded from the closure unless the box for that part is selected.

Translocation and bioconcentration, Equations 7 and 8

Use the same particle unit for all three values. These ratios are reported separately from the closure.

      Scope of the calculation
      
        
      Limits
      
        
      
          
      
            This sheet applies Equations 1 to 9 from Zure, Kuo and Drizo,
            Chemosphere 2024, volume 351, article 141101.
            doi:10.1016/j.chemosphere.2023.141101
          
      
          
      The user must enter each measurement. The sheet does not create a titer.
      
          
      
            Use one unit for the initial spike, the water and the plant totals. Plaque-forming units and gene
            copies are different units. Equation 9 reports viral particles per millilitre. Those particles are
            excluded from the closure unless the part is selected.
          
      
          
      Equation 2 divides by the dilution fraction that is entered. A 1000-fold dilution is entered as 0.001.
      
          
      
            Blank decay is subtracted from the spiked total before closure. This decay is measured in the
            herb-free control described in the paper.
          
      
          
      
            A positive residual is the portion of the adjusted initial amount that is not recovered in the water
            or the plant parts. A negative residual means the water and the plant parts exceed the adjusted
            initial amount.
          
      
          
      
            The MS2 example uses illustrative part totals from the worked example in this repository. Those
            totals are not a new measurement.
          
      
          
      Records remain in this browser. There is no account. Clearing site data deletes the stored records.
